Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

FrameBook ("the app") is operated by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your email address (used for login and account recovery)
  • A password (stored in encrypted form — we never see your plain-text password)

When you use the app, we store the following data on your behalf:

  • Booking details (client name, shoot location, shoot type, shoot date, shoot description)
  • Quote details (pricing, hours, expenses, tax, status)
  • Invoice details (charges, deposit received, payment status)
  • Business configuration (your business name, contact details, invoice prefix, default rates)

We do not collect payment card details. Subscription payments are processed by Stripe, which has its own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the FrameBook service
  • To sync your data across devices and reinstalls
  • To process your subscription via Stripe
  • To contact you about your account if necessary

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not share your data with anyone except as required to operate the service (Supabase for database hosting, Stripe for payment processing, Railway for server hosting).

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Supabase, a cloud database provider. All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to your data is protected by row-level security — only your account can access your bookings, quotes, and invoices.

A copy of your data is also stored locally on your device in the app's private storage.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete all your data at any time using the "Clear All Data" function in the app's Setup tab. This permanently removes all your bookings, quotes, invoices, and configuration from both your device and our servers.

If you cancel your subscription, your data remains stored for 30 days before being deleted, giving you time to export it.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Export your data (via CSV export in the Setup tab)
  • Delete your data (via Clear All Data in the Setup tab)
  • Close your account by contacting us at the email below

6. Third-Party Services

FrameBook uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Stripe — subscription billing and payment processing
  • Railway — server hosting

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing their use of data.

7. Children's Privacy

FrameBook is intended for use by adults running photography businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at:
support@framebook.app
